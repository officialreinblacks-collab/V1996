Market watch

Is crypto falling or rising, and why?

By Mollary J Molly (Researcher and Investor)
Update for 9 November 2026

TL;DR

Crypto has weakened since our last discussion. Total market capitalization is now $2.5T, down 4.64% over seven days and 9.45% over 30 days. Fear has deepened from 33 to 28, and altcoin participation has fallen to an Altcoin Season Index of 40.

The main drivers are renewed ETF outflows, a broad risk-off move in equities, and leverage rebuilding faster than spot demand. This is a defensive market, not a clean recovery.

Spot demand has deteriorated again

US spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs recorded combined outflows of $1.0353B across November 5–6, following a $127.6M inflow on November 4. Bitcoin ETFs accounted for $842.1M of those outflows and Ethereum ETFs $193.2M. Bitcoin ETFs also recorded approximately $1.28B of outflows last week, their weakest weekly result since late June. ETF-flow coverage

This reverses the post-election rebound. A short-lived inflow day was not enough to establish sustained institutional demand.

The market is rotating defensively

Bitcoin dominance is 60.19%, up from 60.02% last week, while the Altcoin Season Index has dropped 16.67% over seven days and 31.03% over 30 days. Capital is therefore holding up relatively better in Bitcoin while altcoins lose participation.

That matches the recent asset-specific flow picture: Solana ETFs reportedly saw no net inflows for 12 consecutive trading sessions, including approximately $25.08M of SOL outflows in the latest reported session. Solana ETF-flow coverage

Leverage is rebuilding into weakness

Global open interest is $355B, up 23.51% over seven days, while average funding is positive at +0.0026813%. Bitcoin liquidations reached $303.03M over 24 hours and $1.22B over seven days.

Leverage has risen from the weekly low even as market capitalization remains under pressure.

That creates squeeze risk: a modest further decline could force long positions to close, while positive funding shows that traders are still paying to maintain bullish exposure.

Macro is still a major drag

Over the market-overview windows, SPY fell 2.27%, QQQ fell 3.78%, and gold rose 4.18%. Crypto’s seven-day correlation was 0.89242 with SPY and 0.78678 with QQQ, versus 0.033687 with gold.

This is a risk-off rotation rather than a crypto-only breakdown. The latest coverage also points to October CPI on November 10, PPI on Friday, and ongoing Federal Reserve commentary as near-term volatility catalysts. Bitcoin market-risk coverage

Bottom line

The evidence has shifted more bearish since our last discussion: weaker market breadth, renewed ETF selling, and high leverage are outweighing the earlier rebound.

The read would improve if:

  • ETF flows turn persistently positive.
  • Open interest declines without another large liquidation wave.
