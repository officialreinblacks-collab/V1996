MARKETPLUSVIEW — MTF STRUCTURE SIGNALS

Higher timeframe BOS / CHOCH sets the bias · the entry timeframe prints the BUY / SELL · one tool, one selector

Analysis tool only. It never sends an order and nothing here promises profit. A signal is a structure event that matched your rules, not a guaranteed result. Always use your own stop loss and risk limit.

1. What it does

You choose a higher structure timeframe (for example 1 Hour) and an entry timeframe (for example 5 Minutes). The tool reads market structure on the higher timeframe to decide the direction (the bias), then waits for the entry timeframe to confirm a matching break. Only then does it print BUY or SELL. It does not matter which timeframe your chart is showing: both timeframes are loaded on their own.

2. Quick start

  1. Add MARKETPLUSVIEW — MTF STRUCTURE SIGNALS to the chart (Indicators → Trend).
  2. Open its settings. At the top choose Strategy mode: BOS — Trend Continuation or CHOCH — Trend Reversal.
  3. Pick the higher timeframe and the entry timeframe. Default: 1 Hour and 5 Minutes.
  4. Press OK. Wait a few seconds while both timeframes load (a yellow message appears at the bottom while loading).
  5. Read the dashboard (top left), then wait for a BUY or SELL arrow after a candle has closed.

That is all a beginner needs. Everything else is optional.

3. BOS mode and CHOCH mode

The two modes are mutually exclusive. Only the selected one is active. Switching resets the signal conditions and the alert memory, so nothing from the previous mode leaks into the new one.

BOS = a break in the direction of the established structure (continuation). CHOCH = a break against the previously established direction (change of character). The very first break after the tool starts reading has no earlier structure, so it is shown as BOS (start) and never creates a signal.

4. Timeframes

Keep the higher timeframe clearly larger than the entry timeframe (for example 1H with 5M, or 4H with 15M). If it is not larger, the tool shows a warning. 2 Minutes and 10 Minutes are built from 1 Minute and 5 Minute candles. Each timeframe loads 1000 candles by default (2000 or 3000 in settings). Older history has no signals.

5. How structure is found

  • A swing high / low is confirmed only after N candles on each side are lower / higher. N is the swing sensitivity: 2, 3, 5, 8, 10 or Custom. A small number reacts fast and gives more noise. A big number gives fewer, cleaner swings. Defaults: 5 on the higher timeframe, 3 on the entry timeframe. Higher and entry sensitivity are separate.
  • Break confirmation: Candle close (recommended) needs a candle to close beyond the level. Wick break accepts a wick. Even in wick mode, Require entry timeframe confirmation keeps the signal until a candle has closed beyond the level.
  • The level that gets broken is the most recent confirmed swing. The label tells you whether it was a BOS or a CHOCH.

9. Alerts

Alerts show as a message while the app is open. Available: Bullish BOS BUY, Bearish BOS SELL, Bullish CHOCH BUY, Bearish CHOCH SELL, higher timeframe bullish structure, higher timeframe bearish structure, higher timeframe bias change, and any new entry signal. Each confirmed signal or higher timeframe event fires once. When you open the tool, change a setting or switch mode, existing history is treated as already seen and does not trigger alerts.

10. No repainting

  • Swing points are confirmed only after the required candles on both sides. Nothing is guessed from the forming candle.
  • Signals are built only from closed entry candles. The forming candle of either timeframe is never used.
  • A higher timeframe break counts only from the moment that higher TF candle has closed. An entry candle never sees a higher TF candle that was still open at that time: no look-ahead.
  • A printed signal does not move or disappear when new candles arrive. If you change a setting, the whole chart is recalculated from the rules you chose; that is a new calculation, not repainting.

11. Good to know

  • Data comes from the exchange feed of the market on your chart (spot or perpetual). Markets without that feed use the chart candles when the chart timeframe divides the selected timeframe (for example a 1 Minute chart can build a 5 Minute entry timeframe). Otherwise the tool shows a message.
  • Close to a market open or after a data gap, a swing needs enough candles to be confirmed. Expect a short wait after loading.
  • More signals do not mean a better tool. Start with the defaults, watch the dashboard, and tighten with filters only when you understand why.
  • Test any setup on history and in paper trading before using real money.
