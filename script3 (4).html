MARKETPLUSVIEW — MTF STRUCTURE SIGNALS

One tool, one selector: pick a higher timeframe for market structure and a lower timeframe for entries. The tool prints BUY / SELL only after a candle has closed and only in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias.

Quick start

  1. Add the tool from Premium Strategy and open its settings.
  2. Choose STRATEGY MODE: BOS (trend continuation) or CHOCH (trend reversal). This is the main setting; the other mode is switched off automatically.
  3. Choose the Higher structure timeframe (default 1 Hour) and the Entry timeframe (default 5 Minutes). The higher timeframe must be larger than the entry timeframe.
  4. Leave the rest on the defaults to start. It works on whatever chart timeframe you are viewing.

The two modes

BOS — Trend Continuation
  • HTF: a candle closes beyond the last swing in the direction of the existing structure = BOS. That sets the bias.
  • Entry: wait for an entry-timeframe BOS the same way. BUY with bullish bias, SELL with bearish bias.
  • If the HTF prints a CHOCH, the bias goes neutral until a new BOS confirms the new direction.
CHOCH — Trend Reversal
  • HTF: a close beyond the swing against the existing structure = CHOCH. That flips the bias.
  • Entry: wait for an entry-timeframe CHOCH in the direction of the new HTF bias.
  • The bias stays until the opposite HTF CHOCH.

Switching the mode re-calculates everything from scratch: old signal conditions and alert history of the other mode are dropped.

How structure is read

  • Confirmed swings only. A swing high / low counts after N candles on each side have closed (Swing sensitivity: 2, 3, 5, 8, 10 or Custom). Larger = fewer, more significant swings.
  • BOS = break in the established direction. CHOCH = break against it. The very first break, when no structure exists yet, is shown as a grey BRK and is never counted as BOS or CHOCH.
  • Break confirmation: candle close (recommended) or wick break.
  • Swings are labelled HH / LH / HL / LL. Dotted grey lines are developing (not yet confirmed) swings; solid markers are confirmed.

Every component has its own on/off switch in the settings.

Filters (all optional)

  • Require higher timeframe confirmation (on by default): a signal needs an HTF bias that matches.
  • Require entry confirmation candle: the break candle must close in the signal direction.
  • Prevent duplicate signals in the same direction (on by default).
  • Signal cooldown in entry candles.
  • Ignore signals when structure is neutral (on by default).
  • Minimum swing size, minimum candle body and ATR volatility filters, each with its own value.

A signal never goes against the HTF bias, whatever the filter settings.

Dashboard

Strategy, Higher TF, Entry TF, HTF Bias, Last HTF Event, Entry Structure (Bullish / Bearish / Waiting), Signal Status (BUY / SELL / WAIT) and the Last Signal with price and time (UTC). Signal Status shows BUY or SELL while the last signal still agrees with the HTF bias, otherwise WAIT.

Alerts

Available: Bullish BOS BUY, Bearish BOS SELL, Bullish CHOCH BUY, Bearish CHOCH SELL, HTF bullish structure, HTF bearish structure, HTF bias change, and any new entry signal. Each confirmed event fires once. Alerts work while the app is open.

No repainting

  • Signals and structure use closed candles only; a candle counts as closed only if its data was read after it ended.
  • The HTF event is known at the close of its candle, never earlier: no look-ahead.
  • Printed signals never move or disappear when new candles arrive.

Good to know

  • HTF and entry candles are loaded on their own, so the chart timeframe does not matter. 2 minutes, 10 minutes and 8 hours are built from 1m, 5m and 4h candles.
  • History depth follows what the data feed provides (up to roughly 1000 candles per request, with extra pages for the entry timeframe). Signals appear only where both timeframes have data.
  • If you see few signals in BOS mode, that is expected: after an HTF CHOCH the bias is neutral until a new BOS. Try a smaller swing sensitivity or the CHOCH mode.
Risk notice. This is a market-structure tool. It does not predict price, and no signal, setting or result is a guarantee of profit. Test it on your own markets and manage your risk.

MARKETPLUSVIEW — SMART MONEY ORDER BLOCK & VOLUME PROFILE (SMOBVP)

Finds order blocks left behind by strong structure breaks, checks each one against a buyer vs seller volume profile, and prints BUY / SELL only when price comes back and rejects a block that the volume agrees with. Every signal comes with a stop, two targets and a results dashboard.

Quick start

  1. Add the tool from Premium Strategy and open its settings.
  2. Pick the Signal filter: Strict, Balanced (default) or Aggressive.
  3. Leave the rest on the defaults to start. It works on any market and timeframe; it needs about 205 candles of history before it starts.

How an order block is made

  • A swing high / low is confirmed after N candles on each side have closed (Swing length, default 5).
  • A strong candle closes beyond that swing and its body is at least Displacement body times the ATR (default 1.0). That is the structure break.
  • The block is the last opposite candle before the break (looking back up to 9 candles): the last red candle for a bullish block, the last green candle for a bearish block. Its height is capped at 1.4 ATR.
  • A block lives up to 60 candles. It is removed when price closes through it, when it is used, or when it gets too old. At most 5 blocks are kept.

The buyer vs seller volume profile

  • The last Profile lookback candles (default 100) are split into 24 price rows. Each row shows how much volume was buying (green) and selling (red) pressure. A candle that closes near its high counts mostly as buying.
  • The row with the most volume is the POC (point of control, yellow line).
  • Each block gets two numbers from the rows it covers: how heavy that volume is compared with the POC, and the defender pressure — the share of buying volume for a bullish block, or of selling volume for a bearish block. The % in the block label is the defender pressure.

When a BUY / SELL prints

BUY
  • Price trades back into a bullish block on a later candle.
  • The candle closes green, at or above the middle of the block, with a long lower wick (at least 45% of its range).
  • The volume filter and the trend filter agree (below).
SELL
  • Price trades back into a bearish block on a later candle.
  • The candle closes red, at or below the middle of the block, with a long upper wick (at least 45% of its range).
  • The volume filter and the trend filter agree (below).

The first rejection of a block uses it up, signal or not. A block is never traded twice. Only one trade is open at a time, and no new trade can start on the candle where the last one closed.

  • Trend + POC filter (on by default): BUY only above the 200 EMA and not far below the POC; SELL only below the 200 EMA and not far above the POC.

Stop, targets and results

  • Stop: just beyond the block or the signal candle (whichever is further), plus 0.35 ATR. A signal is skipped if the risk is smaller than 0.3 ATR or bigger than 3.5 ATR.
  • T1 (default 1.5R): half of the position is taken. T2 (default 3R): the rest is taken and the trade ends. 1R is the distance from entry to stop.
  • If the stop is hit after T1, the result is the half profit minus the half loss (+0.25R with the defaults). A full stop is −1R.
  • Time exit: if neither target nor stop is reached within 80 candles, the trade is closed at the candle close.
  • On a candle that touches both the stop and a target, the stop counts first (conservative).

Dashboard

Preset, Signal (LONG ACTIVE / SHORT ACTIVE / WAITING), Nearest OB Defense, POC, Data and Trades / Win / Net R. The position and size can be changed in the settings, or the dashboard can be switched off.

  • Data = REAL VOL: the market provides volume. PRICE PROXY: no volume (for example some synthetic indices), so the candle range is used instead.
  • Trades / Win / Net counts finished trades only. It is a record of what the rules did on the loaded candles, not a promise of future results.

Alerts

BUY signal, SELL signal and trade exit (T2, stop or time exit). Each event fires once, after its candle has closed. Alerts work while the app is open.

No repainting

  • Blocks, signals and trades use closed candles only. A printed signal never moves or disappears.
  • Only the volume profile, the POC and the dashboard follow the candle that is still forming.

Good to know

  • Signals are rare on purpose: a block must survive the volume filter, the trend filter and a clean rejection candle. Try Aggressive or switch the trend filter off to see more.
  • Shorter lookback reacts faster; longer lookback gives a steadier POC. Swing length 5 is a good middle ground.
  • Pine Screener and Data Window outputs of the original script are not part of this tool.
